# Approval for Exceptions and Uncertain Designs

- Stop affected work and obtain my explicit approval before deviating from
  applicable standards, best practices, clean modular design, or project guidance.
  This includes temporary workarounds and technical debt.
- Never build substantial code on uncertain architecture. First investigate or
  use small, isolated, reversible experiments. If assumptions still risk major
  rework, correctness, security, or operation, obtain approval before proceeding.
- Explain the applicable rule or conflict, evidence, uncertainty, scope,
  alternatives, and risks. Separate requirements from preferences. Recommend
  the simplest approach, validation, recovery, and any temporary exception's
  removal condition. Include maintenance and testing costs.
- Continue safe investigation and independent work while awaiting approval.
  Silence, urgency, tool limits, existing violations, and general permission
  to finish do not authorize exceptions.
- Document approved decisions and limits. Reuse approval within those limits;
  seek new approval when scope, assumptions, or risks materially change.
  Approval cannot override higher-priority instructions.
